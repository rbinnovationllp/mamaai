# Customer Onboarding Flow

1. Register or sign in.
2. A new household is offered **What to Cook Today?** before payment.
3. The customer can generate and reopen one saved free day; skipping does not consume it.
4. To subscribe, the customer completes the permanent family profile. Free-plan details are brought in as editable, unsaved prefill.
5. Every human member needs an age before paid checkout. Seven-day history must be completed or marked “Do not remember.”
6. MAMAAI recommends Starter (1–4), Premium (5–6), or Family Plus (7+) and Razorpay checkout validates the choice server-side.

Document/profile imports remain review-first: extract → editable review → customer approval → normal profile save. No upload silently replaces saved details.
