# Approved Subscription Pricing

## India (new enrolment)

| Plan | Human members | Monthly price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Family Starter / Small Family | 1–4 | ₹399 |
| Family Premium / Medium Family | 5–6 | ₹599 |
| Family Plus / Large Family | 7+ | ₹999 |

Pets do not count toward human member limits. Family Plus has no product-imposed 10-person ceiling. Existing paid subscribers must retain their agreed billing until an approved migration exists.

## International

No change: Starter US$4.99, Premium US$7.99, and Family Plus US$12.99 monthly. International Razorpay mappings and existing customer billing are not modified by this work.

Checkout server validation blocks a plan smaller than the saved human household, and requires profile ages plus seven-day history or an explicit “Do not remember” response. GST treatment and live plan activation remain owner/payment-provider verification gates.
