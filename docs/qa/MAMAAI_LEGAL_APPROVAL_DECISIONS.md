# RBIL legal-policy decisions required

These are approval decisions, not implemented legal terms. No account deletion execution, refund entitlement, or production policy publication is authorized until RBIL approves the relevant text with qualified legal advice.

## ISS-006 — account deletion and retention

RBIL must approve:

1. The identity re-verification method required before a deletion request may execute.
2. Which customer data is deleted, anonymised, retained or placed on legal hold.
3. Retention periods for subscription/payment records, security/audit logs, support records and backups.
4. Whether deletion is immediate or processed after a stated review window, and the customer notification wording.
5. The authorized support escalation and exception process.

Until then, MAMAAI must keep deletion as a verified preflight/request only and must not claim automatic erasure.

**Draft wording for legal review (not approved):** “After we verify your identity and review any legal retention obligations, we will delete or anonymise eligible personal data. We may retain the minimum data required by law, for fraud prevention, security, dispute handling and backup-rotation periods. We will tell you the outcome and any lawful retention exception.”

## ISS-012 — refund and cancellation policy

RBIL must approve:

1. Eligible plans, cancellation effective date and whether service continues through the paid period.
2. Refund eligibility, calculation method, exclusions, GST treatment and any statutory consumer rights.
3. Request channel, evidence requirements, response timeframe and escalation contact.
4. The final customer-facing English/Hindi policy text.

Until then, MAMAAI must not process or promise refunds beyond the unapproved draft/disclosure.

**Draft wording for legal review (not approved):** “Cancellation and refund eligibility depend on your selected plan, the applicable consumer law and the policy shown at purchase. RBIL will state the request channel, decision timeframe, any refund calculation, GST treatment and statutory rights before accepting paid subscriptions.”

## ISS-013 — terms of service

RBIL must approve:

1. Contracting entity, service scope, eligibility and account/customer responsibilities.
2. Acceptable use, subscription, cancellation, limitation-of-liability and dispute-resolution clauses.
3. Food/health disclaimer alignment and the relationship to the privacy and refund policies.
4. Governing law, contact details, effective date, change-notice process and English/Hindi precedence.

Until then, the route must remain a draft/disclosure and must not be represented as executed legal terms.

**Draft wording for legal review (not approved):** “These Terms govern use of MAMAAI once approved and published by Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP. They will identify the service scope, customer responsibilities, subscription terms, safety limitations, privacy terms, dispute process, governing law and the effective date. The final published version will control.”
