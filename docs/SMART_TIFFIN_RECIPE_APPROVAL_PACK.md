## 1. `paneer-sandwich` — Paneer vegetable sandwich

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 4 bread slices; 120 g paneer; 80 g tomato; 80 g cucumber; 30 g curd.
- **Instructions:** Crumble or grate paneer, combine with curd and chopped vegetables, assemble between bread slices, then toast or grill if desired.
- **Times:** preparation 10 minutes; cooking/toasting 10 minutes; active total 20 minutes.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** none.
- **Allergens:** milk, gluten.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only after allergy, age and school-food-policy checks.
- **Packing/storage:** cool the filling before packing; use an insulated bag for the perishable filling. Do not represent this as safe at ambient temperature for a specified duration.
- **Uncertainties:** bread slice weight/brand, exact toasting method and storage duration require review.

## 2. `roti-roll` — Paneer bhurji roti roll

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 2 prepared rotis; 150 g paneer; 60 g onion; 80 g tomato; 10 ml oil.
- **Instructions:** Sauté onion and tomato in oil, crumble in paneer and cook through, fill the prepared rotis, then roll and cut if needed.
- **Times:** preparation 5 minutes; cooking 10 minutes; active total 15 minutes. Even with prepared rotis, this is not a 10-minute option.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** 2 prepared rotis.
- **Allergens:** milk, gluten.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only after allergy, age and packing-policy checks.
- **Packing/storage:** allow filling to cool slightly before wrapping; do not make a long ambient-holding safety claim.
- **Uncertainties:** the source includes dough preparation/rest; the exact prepared-roti size and cooking time need review.

## 3. `curd-rice` — Curd rice with cucumber

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 300 g cooked, cooled rice; 250 g curd; 100 g cucumber; 5 ml oil; 2 g mustard seeds.
- **Instructions:** Mix cooled rice, curd and chopped cucumber. Temper mustard seeds in oil if that optional step is approved, cool the tempering, then combine.
- **Times:** preparation 5 minutes; cooking 0 minutes for basic assembly; active total 5 minutes. It is within 10 minutes only with cooked, cooled rice already available.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** 300 g cooked, cooled rice.
- **Allergens:** milk, mustard.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only where cold-chain/insulated-packing capability and individual dietary checks are satisfied.
- **Packing/storage:** use an insulated container with a cold pack where refrigeration is unavailable. No room-temperature safety duration is asserted.
- **Uncertainties:** source total time is longer; optional garnish is excluded and the tempering/seasoning quantities need review.

## 4. `besan-chilla` — Vegetable besan chilla

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 100 g besan; 60 g onion; 60 g tomato; 150 ml water; 10 ml oil.
- **Instructions:** Whisk besan and water into a smooth batter, fold in chopped vegetables, cook thin chillas on an oiled pan, and turn until cooked.
- **Times:** preparation 10 minutes; cooking 10 minutes; active total 20 minutes. It is not a genuine 10-minute option.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** none.
- **Allergens:** none identified in the listed ingredients; check individual legume sensitivity and shared-kitchen cross-contact separately.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only after individual dietary and school-policy checks.
- **Packing/storage:** pack after steam dissipates to limit sogginess; eat warm where practical.
- **Uncertainties:** water and spice adjustment may vary; spices are excluded from aggregation pending review.

## 5. `idli-stir-fry` — Idli stir-fry

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 6 prepared idlis; 60 g onion; 80 g tomato; 10 ml oil.
- **Instructions:** Cut prepared idlis into bite-size pieces, sauté onion and tomato in oil, add idli pieces and stir until heated through.
- **Times:** preparation 4 minutes; cooking 6 minutes; active total 10 minutes only with already prepared idlis. Making idlis from soaked/fermented batter is not included in this claim.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** 6 prepared idlis.
- **Allergens:** none identified in the listed ingredients; validate the prepared idli’s ingredients separately.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only after age, ingredient and school-policy checks.
- **Packing/storage:** pack dry; cut pieces for younger children and observe school restrictions.
- **Uncertainties:** FSSAI reference covers idli preparation from scratch; prepared-idli weight and base ingredients require review.

## 6. `vegetable-poha` — Vegetable poha

- **Version/status:** draft-v1 — DRAFT — AWAITING RBIL REVIEW
- **Servings:** 2
- **Ingredients:** 100 g poha; 60 g onion; 60 g peas; 10 ml oil; 10 ml lemon juice.
- **Instructions:** Rinse poha briefly and drain, sauté onion and peas in oil, fold in poha until heated, then add lemon juice after cooking.
- **Times:** preparation 10 minutes; cooking 5 minutes; active total 15 minutes. It is not a genuine 10-minute option.
- **Preprepared ingredients:** none.
- **Allergens:** none identified in the listed ingredients; check product-specific cross-contact labels.
- **Suitability:** school and office tiffin candidate only after individual dietary, age and packing-policy checks.
- **Packing/storage:** pack warm in a vented insulated container after cooking; no holding-time guarantee is made.
- **Uncertainties:** optional spices and garnish are excluded from aggregation; pea form and poha thickness affect preparation time.
