# MAMAAI Phase 3B.3 privacy and legal owner decisions

RBIL decisions required:

- Refunds: eligibility/window, cancellation timing, billing disclosure, taxes, failed/duplicate-charge process. Recommendation: keep payments disabled until counsel-approved wording exists.
- Terms: contracting entity, law, service scope, acceptable use, AI limitations, suspension/termination and subscription terms. Recommendation: retain draft notice until final text is supplied.
- Privacy: family/allergy/diet categories, purposes, storage region/providers, consent/withdrawal, analytics, export/deletion and retention. Recommendation: keep non-essential analytics disabled until a bilingual consent model is approved.
- Deletion: verification factor, revoke sessions, household/pantry/meal deletion, payment and audit exceptions, backups and legal retention. Recommendation: keep deletion non-executable until policy and a user-session lookup design are approved.

No legally binding claim is created here.
